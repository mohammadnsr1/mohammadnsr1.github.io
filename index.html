About

I am a robotics PhD student at Arizona State University working on embodied and agentic AI with direct applications in industry. My background is in mechanical engineering, with earlier work in dynamics and vibration and in acoustics.

I work on:

  • Perception and active perception for mobile robots
  • Manipulation, digital twins, and kinematics
  • Bio-inspired acoustic sensing
  • Nonlinear dynamics and automated experimental testing
